    What I bring to a team

    I find out why people respond the way they do

    My Ph.D. in communication science gave me solid research tools, quantitative and qualitative. I turn behavioral, survey, and interview data into insights people can act on and understand.

    I connect social science and engineering

    As research coordinator at the Center for Health and Risk Communication, I coordinated six interdisciplinary research groups. On an NIH project, I worked with a computer science team and translated between social science and code.

    I take ownership and move fast

    I learn fast and keep myself at the cutting edge. I take ownership of my part, move quickly, and speak clearly and work closely with different groups.
